# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

This privacy policy explains how personal data is handled in the Formwork app for iPhone ("the app") and on this website.

**In short:** the app doesn't send your data to us. Everything you enter stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. The app contains no tracking, analytics or advertising.

## Who is responsible

The controller responsible for data processing is Daniel Wolbach. You'll find the full postal address in the [Imprint](/legal/imprint). You can reach us by email at [contact@formworkfitness.com](mailto:contact@formworkfitness.com).

## App

### Data stored on your device

The exercises, workouts, sessions and statistics you create in the app are stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data and don't receive it. If you delete the app, this data is deleted from your device.

### iCloud sync

If iCloud is enabled for Formwork on your device, your data is synced via Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and stored in your private iCloud database. Only you can access it; we can't. This processing is carried out by Apple, Inc. as described in [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can turn off iCloud sync for Formwork at any time in the iOS Settings app under _[Your Name] › iCloud_.

### Camera

The app asks for access to your camera only so you can scan QR codes. The camera image is processed on your device solely to read the code. It isn't saved and isn't sent to us or anyone else. You can deny or withdraw camera access at any time in the iOS Settings app under _Formwork_.

### Apple Health

You can connect Formwork to Apple Health (HealthKit), but you don't have to. Before the app accesses any health data, iOS asks for your permission and lets you choose which data types to allow.

With your permission, the app:

- **saves your sessions as workouts** in Apple Health once you finish them. If you delete a session in the app, the matching workout is removed from Apple Health too.
- **reads your heart rate** while a session is running, to show it live during your workout.
- **reads and saves your body weight and body fat percentage** so you can follow them as graphs in your statistics and log new measurements.

The health data the app reads is processed on your device. Health data is never sent to us, never shared with third parties and never used for advertising.

Health data is a special category of personal data under the GDPR. It is processed only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give in the iOS permission dialog. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the iOS Settings app under _Privacy & Security › Health › Formwork_. Data the app already saved in Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

### No tracking, analytics or advertising

The app contains no tracking, analytics or advertising tools and doesn't send any data to us or to third parties.

### App Store

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes data related to downloading and purchasing the app under its own responsibility and [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Formwork Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Apple handles the purchase, payment, renewals, cancellations and refunds; we don't receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. To unlock Premium, the app checks your subscription status with Apple directly on your device. This information isn't sent to us. We only see aggregated sales figures that don't identify you. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the iOS Settings app under _[Your Name] › Subscriptions_.

If you've turned on _Share With App Developers_ (iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple shares anonymized crash reports and usage statistics with us. We use them only to fix bugs and improve the app.

## Website

This website and our email are hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. Hetzner processes data only in data centers in Germany and on our behalf, under a data processing agreement in accordance with Art. 28 GDPR. For details, see [Hetzner's privacy policy](https://www.hetzner.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

When you visit the website, your browser automatically sends technical information to the server, including your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested, the referring website and your browser and operating system. This information is needed to deliver the website and is stored in server log files to keep it secure, for example to detect and prevent attacks. The log files are deleted after 7 days.

This processing is based on our legitimate interest in presenting the website reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### No cookies or tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts and images are served from this website itself, so visiting it doesn't connect you to Google or other third parties.

### Email

If you email us, we process your email address and the content of your message solely to answer you. This is based on our legitimate interest in responding to your inquiry (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete your message once it has been dealt with, unless we're legally required to keep it.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
- have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16)
- have your data deleted (Art. 17)
- restrict how your data is processed (Art. 18)
- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
- object to processing based on our legitimate interests (Art. 21)

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above. Since we don't receive any data from the app, this mostly concerns messages you've sent us.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Rhineland-Palatinate [www.datenschutz.rlp.de](https://www.datenschutz.rlp.de/).

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this privacy policy when the app or website changes in a way that affects your data, for example when new features are added. The current version is always available on this page.
